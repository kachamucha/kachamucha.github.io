This issue begins with the Prisoner's Dilemma, before zooming out to understand the LPG crisis in India and around the world through the Strait of Hormuz blockade, war, and the supply chains that keep everyday essentials moving. We then turn inward, asking what happens when light sees itself for the first time through reflection, and use that strange idea to explore perception, identity, and the gap between how we see ourselves, how others see us, and who we actually are. From there, decode the remarkable efficiency of Mumbai's dabbawala system, before stepping into the colourful world of Andy Warhol and his impact on the landscape of art, followed by the surprising mathematical pattern hiding in nature: the Fibonacci sequence. We end with planned obsolescence through the stories of the iPod and the humble lightbulb, before exploring a more hopeful possibility: shared economies, including Bengaluru startup GroClub's cycle-rental model. And the curiosity doesn't have to end when you close the mag. Scan selected articles to find personalised AI's, designed to nudge you further into the subject if you are curious to find out more.
