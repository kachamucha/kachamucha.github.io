Issue 01  Befizuli Sawaal

This issue dives into the inner workings of industrial machines and explores how climate shapes our bodies. From there, we enter the world of stock markets with a look at the dot-com bubble. Ever wondered whether the brain and the mind are actually different? We unpack that question next. We then journey into space to explore neutron stars, before examining the interception geometry behind predation. Closer to home, we look at dry and wet waste, and the people whose work keeps our cities running. We end with a futures-thinking piece by Begum Rokeya, Sultana's Dream, an alternate history where gender roles are reversed, women hold power, and society is built around appreciating nature's gifts to humanity.
