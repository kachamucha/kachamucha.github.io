How Befizuli Sawal started?

Purely stewed from frustration of the education system and its touchpoints not being engaging enough, especially for a nurodivergent audience. These touchpoints have remained the same for decades and with more screens around us keeping us engrossed, coming back to plain old books with one sad illustration is just sad at this point. I took action on the frustration during my pre thesis project at srishti manipal, and I have been working out ways to enter this wicked system ever since.

What is Kacha Mucha ?

We cook visually appetizing magazines, that pushes older teens down rabbit holes about how the world works, while holding a space for perspectives that extend beyond the syllabus.

Wait, what does Kacha Mucha mean ?

Kacha Mucha means scribbling or rough drawing in Tamil. Visual learners often grasp concepts more easily when information is visual and color-coded. The mag is inspired by the colourful notes we made as kids.

About the founder

Hi, I'm Shubh.

I'm a creative educator. That's code for, I use visual design to tell stories, make ideas easier to understand, and hopefully spark a little curiosity along the way.

Over the years, I've had the opportunity to exhibit my work at the Lalit Kala Akademi, spend a brief minute teaching at Tapmi, Manipal, NID (National Institute of Design), and work alongside farming communities to help bring their stories to urban audiences. I tend to think of design not just as something that looks aesthetic, but also as a tool for social impact.

Kacha Mucha is an extension of that belief.
